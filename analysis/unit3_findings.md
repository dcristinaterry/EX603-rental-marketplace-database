UNIT 3 - FINDINGS

The dashboard reported that approximately 50% of scheduled property viewings resulted in renters not showing up. Based on this report, management decided to freeze agent commission payments. However, after reviewing the viewing records, I found that the reported percentage needs further verification. My investigation identified 44 confirmed renter no-shows out of 200 total eligible viewings. 

I found two fields, one viewing_status and another, no_show_reason.  When running an initial search by status of cancelled, the search returned only 14 records, while the corrected search returned 156 records that attended. Some of the 44 showed disputed events, but we can see records showing viewings after the disputed event was created.  This demonstrates how missing information can affect the results.

However, a missing reason does not necessarily mean the renter attended or failed to attend, so these records should be reviewed separately.  For example I found that out of the 44 records some records with cancellations of “Renter No Show”, had viewings after missing that specific viewing so the commission for the agent could be revised.

With all the different findings I recommend management reconsider the commission freeze while the dashboard results are verified.
